How to read this tracker & source notes

Advancement records come from Troop0154FIAR_20261111.csv. The layout follows scout tracker T154.xlsx: requirements down the page and Scouts across the columns. Patrol assignments follow the supplied lists. Theo is Theodore Rabeling; Findlay is Findlay Rae Garrett. Felix is assigned to Gladiators. Alexander and all remaining boys are assigned to Barbarians. Allison (Fluffy), Riley and Carson (Gladiators) were added manually as new Scouts with no dates or awarded ranks. Their undated requirements are included in patrol progress totals and missing-requirement lists.

The export’s “Current rank” field repeats Scout (04/14/2026) for every person. This tracker instead uses dated rank section headings. For a rank already earned, an undated requirement is shown green, not missing. A dated higher rank also covers earlier ranks. Dates shown in the cells retain the source’s MM/DD/YYYY format.

A missing mark means no completion date was recorded; it does not establish whether a Scout has performed the task. Parent summary rows with lettered subrequirements are excluded to avoid double-counting. Merit-badge sublists are not expanded here; the numbered merit-badge requirement is retained. Counts describe individual requirement entries, not official advancement percentages. Requirement wording is the abbreviated wording in the supplied export.

This self-contained HTML works offline. Advancement data is a read-only snapshot; the training calendar is editable and does not update official advancement records. Export missing CSV and Print view use the current patrol, rank, name, and earned-rank filters. Unknown requirements are excluded from missing counts and exports. No birthdates or membership IDs are included.
